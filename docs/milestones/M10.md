## In plain words

### Starting with what tenants already owe

- **Opening balance per tenant.** On the tenant page, **Add opening balance**: the amount the tenant still owed when you started on LeaseDesk, and the date it was owed on. It is the first row of the tenant's ledger and shows in Unpaid and ageing and on Today. It is never numbered, never sent and carries no GST: those invoices are already in your old books.
- **It is settled like an invoice.** A payment is applied to it first if it is the oldest; bank matching suggests it; "Remind all" and the WhatsApp link ask for it, quoting "Opening balance". The automatic reminder emails never chase it (D55): it is usually old and may still be under discussion, so a person decides.
- **A wrong one can be corrected.** It can be changed until a payment is applied to it. After that, reverse the payment, correct the balance, and record the payment again. It can't be removed once any payment has touched it.
- **It can be imported.** The tenants and leases import has "Opening balance (₹)" and "Balance as on" columns.

### Deposits already held

- A lease that started before LeaseDesk shows **Record ₹X already held** on its page: one tap records the agreed deposit as received on the lease start. The lease import has a "Deposit held (₹)" column. It can't be recorded twice or on a renewal.
- **The exit statement counts a tenant's unused advance once.** A tenant with two leases used to have their advance counted on both statements. Now it is shared out across their leases, oldest dues first, and the opening balance and any credit left over go on the statement of the lease that ends last.

### Setting up in a day

- **Leases | Buildings tabs** at the top of Leases, so buildings, units and the floor plan are one tap away.
- **"Set up LeaseDesk" checklist on Today**, for owners, until it is done: business details, buildings and units, tenants and leases (or import), check one rent schedule, opening balances, invite your team. Each step links to its screen and ticks itself from your data; where it can't tell, you tick it ("Yes, the schedule is right", "Nobody owes anything" or "The rest owe nothing", "Just me for now"). The opening balances step is done only when every tenant has one or you say the rest owe nothing. The checklist disappears when every step is done.
- **Import templates with examples.** The two templates on Leases → Import from Excel have two sample rows each, and each column heading carries its rule in a note.

### Trusting the app with your data

- **Settings → Export all data** (owners only): one zip with an Excel file for each kind of record (your organization and businesses with GSTIN and bank details, buildings, units, tenants, leases, invoices and lines, credit notes, payments, deposits, TDS), every stored invoice PDF and every uploaded document, and a "Read me" explaining the units. Values are exactly as stored (amounts in paise), so nothing is lost by rounding (D57). Each export is logged.
- **Support on every error page** and on **Settings → Help**: WhatsApp +91 98111 29108 and support@leasedesk.com (D54). They're written in one file, `src/lib/support.ts`.
- **Load test.** A made-up organization with 120 tenants, 3 GSTINs and 36 months of invoices and payments. Every screen's data loads in under 2 seconds; Today was 3 seconds before two database indexes were added. The timings are below.

### Smaller fixes

- "Remind all N" counts only tenants with an email, and the confirmation names who has none.
- Plainer wording and better phone layouts on leases, tenants, buildings, billing, payments, bank matching, TDS, log in, sign up, password reset and Settings.

## How to try it

1. Start Docker Desktop. Run `supabase start`, `pnpm db:migrate`, then `pnpm db:seed:demo` and note the password it prints. Run `pnpm build && EMAIL_DRIVER=console pnpm start` and log in at http://localhost:3000 as `demo@sharma-estates.example`.
2. **Opening balance:** Tenants → any tenant → **Add opening balance**, type an amount and a date, **Save opening balance**. Tap **See the ledger**: it is the first row. It shows on Today and in Reports → Unpaid and ageing. Tap **Record payment** on the tenant page and see the payment settle the opening balance first.
3. **Deposit already held:** open a lease that has an agreed deposit but nothing recorded; tap **Record ₹X already held**, then download the deposit settlement statement.
4. **Setup checklist:** sign up as a new organization (log out, **Sign up**). Today shows "Set up LeaseDesk" with six steps; follow the links. On the demo it shows only the steps not yet done.
5. **Leases | Buildings:** tap Leases, then the **Buildings** tab.
6. **Templates:** Leases → **Import from Excel** → **Download the tenants and leases template (.xlsx)**; open it and read the sample rows and the note on each heading.
7. **Export:** Settings → **Export all data** → **Download all data (.zip)**; unzip it and open the Excel files and `files/`.
8. **Support:** Settings → **Help**.
9. **Load test (optional, about 6 minutes):** `pnpm db:seed:load`. It prints the timings; `pnpm db:seed:load --time-only` times again. It replaces only "Load Test Estates", not the demo.

## Fixed after review

The billing and UX reviewers found these on 2026-10-24; all are fixed:

- **A wrong opening balance was locked for good** once a payment was applied, though the message said to reverse the payment. After a reversal it can now be corrected (`drizzle/0036`); it can't be removed once any payment touched it (billing review).
- **"Opening balance" in a bank line's description picked a tenant** in matching. It no longer does (billing review).
- **The business step of the checklist needed a GSTIN,** so a landlord not registered for GST couldn't finish setup. Any business now counts (UX review).
- **"Remind all"'s "Email N reminders"** is the filled button and takes focus; smaller wording fixes: "Yes, remove them", "Yes, the schedule is right", "Send N selected invoices", Floor optional, TDS chips keep the search (UX review).

Codex found these in its two rounds; all are fixed:

- **Today's reminder picked an invoice over the opening balance on a same-day tie,** while payments settle the opening balance first. They now agree (round 1).

- **Export all data left out the organization and its businesses,** so the GSTIN and bank details were missing (round 2, D-036, commit b165fea).

## Backlog (`docs/BACKLOG.md`)

Nothing here gives a wrong amount on an invoice. The M10 items, all dated 2026-10-24:

- **Exit statement:** the credit it counts for each lease can differ from what "Apply credit" would actually apply when TDS is expected on two leases; a renewed lease's statement doesn't include the earlier version's deposit and unpaid invoices.
- **Tally:** a receipt against an opening balance goes in "On Account" (totals still balance, but the old bill stays open in Tally), and a per-business file leaves out a payment applied only to an opening balance. Check with the pilot accountant's sample file.
- **Import:** "Deposit held (₹)" isn't checked against the agreed deposit, so a typo of an extra zero is recorded; a lease ended early can't use "Record ₹X already held".
- **Opening balance:** shows "overdue" on its own as-on day (cosmetic).
- **UI:** the setup checklist fills a phone screen; one name for the invoice contact; bank import wording; small tap targets; empty-state wording on Leases; Business details and Export copy; the ledger's "Balance now"; payment form hints; lease page deposit wording; import heading wording; an unpaid column on the tenant list.
- **Speed:** Today and Unpaid and ageing work tenant by tenant (1.4 s at 120 tenants); fine for the pilot size, the `openInvoiceRows` row says what to do past a few hundred tenants.

## Load test (2026-10-24)

`pnpm db:seed:load` builds "Load Test Estates" on the local database through the app's own code (like the demo, D43): 3 businesses (GSTINs in Maharashtra, Karnataka and Haryana), 3 buildings of 40 units, 120 tenants with one lease each, and 37 months of billing (36 past months and this one): 4,440 invoices sent and 4,284 payments with TDS. Every tenth tenant hasn't paid the last three months, and nobody has paid this month yet. Seeding took 344 seconds on a MacBook. It then times, as the owner, the same functions each screen calls, twice (the second run has warm database caches); `pnpm db:seed:load --time-only` times the organization already there.

What is timed is the server's data work for each screen, not the browser's rendering, which adds little on these pages (no screen draws more than a few hundred rows).

| Report: TDS summary | 18 / 18 | 19 / 18 |

**Fixed:** Today was over 2 seconds on a first load. Working out what each tenant owes looks up the TDS recorded against each invoice (and, since M10, against the opening balance), and `tds_records` had no index on either column, so each of the 120 tenants scanned the table. `drizzle/0035` adds both indexes.

**Still the slowest:** Today and Unpaid and ageing (about 1.4 s) work out what is owed one tenant at a time (`openInvoiceRows`: settings, invoices, payments, credit notes and TDS per tenant). They are under the 2-second bar at 120 tenants but grow with the number of tenants; doing it in one query per kind for all tenants is the next step if a landlord has a few hundred (already in `docs/BACKLOG.md`, the `openInvoiceRows` row).
