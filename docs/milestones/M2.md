# M2. Data, auth and setup screens: what was built

Finished 2026-10-23, including the billing and UX reviews. Next up is **M3: leases**.

## In plain words

A landlord can now create an account, set up their business and buildings, and record their tenants. Nothing is billed yet: leases come in M3 and invoices in M4.

- **Accounts.** Sign up with an email and password; the account becomes the owner of a new organization. Log in and out. If sign-up stops halfway, the app asks for the business name next time.
- **Team.** The owner invites people by email and role (Owner, Accounts, Property manager, Viewer) and gets a one-time link to send, by copy and paste or WhatsApp (the app doesn't email it yet). Each role can only change what the product plan allows; the database refuses everything else too. Someone who opens a form they can't save sees who can change it and a way back, instead of a form that fails.
- **Business details.** Name, legal name and PAN, plus each GSTIN invoices are issued from, with its address, bank account and UPI ID. GSTINs are checked for typing mistakes (the check digit) and must match the chosen state.
- **Billing rules.** Every billing, GST and TDS behaviour that landlords do differently is a setting with a safe default, shown in plain words with an example: "₹1,10,250 + 5% = ₹1,15,762.50. To the rupee, that becomes ₹1,15,763." Rupee amounts use Indian grouping (₹1,00,000). Most rules sit under "More options". Each building can have its own GST rate, SAC code, payment terms and billing timing.
- **Buildings and units** (under Leases). Add buildings, floors and units with carpet and billed area; search and filter units. A **stacking plan** draws each building floor by floor, with each unit's status written out (not only as a colour).
- **Tenants.** Add tenants with GSTIN, PAN, billing address, how they deduct TDS (rent, maintenance (CAM), parking and other charges separately) and contacts with WhatsApp. Search by name, GSTIN, contact or phone; the list says "Deducts TDS" or "No TDS" for each.
- **When something goes wrong.** The app has a plain error page with "Try again" and a "page not found" page, instead of a technical message.
- **Behind the scenes.** Every change is written to an activity log that can't be edited or deleted. One organization can never see another's data: the database enforces it as well as the app. The billing engine now takes all its settings in one object, and every option of every billing setting has its own test.

## How to try it

1. Start Docker Desktop, then `supabase start`, `pnpm db:reset` and `pnpm dev`.
2. Open http://localhost:3000 and **Create an account**.
3. Profile icon → **Settings** → **Business details** → **Add a GSTIN** (try `27AAPFU0939F1ZV`, then change its last letter to see the typing-mistake check).
4. **Leases** → **Buildings and units** → **Add building**; add a floor and a few units; open the **Stacking plan**.
5. **Tenants** → **Add tenant**. Open **More options** to set TDS on maintenance (CAM) differently from rent.
6. **Settings** → **Team** → create an invite link and open it in a private window. Join as a Viewer and try to open a building's edit page: you'll see who can change it instead of the form.
7. **Settings** → **Billing rules**: change a rule, then open a building's defaults.

The seeded demo organization (2 buildings, 50 units, 40 tenants) has no login yet; sign up to create your own.

## Tests added

- 386 unit tests in total (175 at the end of M1). New ones cover:
  - row-level security across all tables, the role table matching the database policies, and the append-only activity log (against the local database)
  - settings: the schema against `docs/SETTINGS.md`, resolution order, storage, the Billing rules form, and building defaults on a new charge (including a blank SAC code)
  - every option of every billing, GST and TDS setting, with hand calculations; with GST rounded once per invoice, a credit note listing its lines in another order puts the rounding paisa on the same line as its invoice
  - GSTIN, PAN, IFSC, UPI and phone checks, invites, and sign-up
- 10 browser tests, each at phone (375px) and laptop (1280px) widths: nav, sign-up and login (3), invites, business details, billing rules, buildings and units, stacking plan, tenants (including clearing a search).

## Settings added

All of `docs/SETTINGS.md`'s billing, GST and TDS settings are now built and on the Billing rules page: rounding of increased rent and invoice totals, part-quarter method, month-end rule, one or two lines for a mid-month increase, ₹0 invoices, building defaults for timing, GST rate and SAC code, due days, GST rounding, TDS rounding, TDS tolerance and TDS quarter. Reminders, alerts and invoice numbering are defined but appear when their features are built.

## For the next milestone (M3)

- Leases get `settings` jsonb and the lease level of `resolveSettings`; `store.ts` takes lease settings as raw jsonb until then.
- New charges must go through `withBuildingDefaults` (`src/lib/settings/charge-defaults.ts`) when a lease is saved (D16), and the result checked with Zod (backlog).
- New tables: composite foreign keys with `organization_id`, then `select app.apply_org_policies(...)` in a custom migration; `rls.db.test.ts` and `roles-rls.db.test.ts` fail otherwise.
- Server actions: `guardedForm(permission, schema, form, fn)` in `src/server/actions/guard.ts`, writes through `insertLogged`/`updateLogged` inside `runScoped`. Pages with a form wrap it in `RequirePermission` (`src/components/shell/RequirePermission.tsx`).
- Forms: `SimpleForm` (`src/components/forms/SimpleForm.tsx`). Next.js keeps the previous page hidden in the DOM, so e2e locators need `{ exact: true }` or `.filter({ visible: true })`.
- The stacking plan and tenant page have placeholders for tenant names and leases.
