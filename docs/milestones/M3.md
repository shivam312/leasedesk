# M3. Leases: what was built

Finished 2026-10-23, including the billing and UX reviews. Next up is **M4: invoicing**.

## In plain words

A landlord can now enter a lease once and see exactly what it will bill, month by month. Nothing is invoiced yet: invoices come in M4.

- **Lease list** (under Leases). Every lease with its tenant, building, dates and status (Active, Leaving, Ended, Ended early, Renewed). Search by tenant or building, and show ended leases with one tap.
- **Add a lease in four steps.** 1) Tenant and units, 2) Dates (start, end, lock-in, notice period, rent-free months or days), 3) Rent and charges (rent, maintenance (CAM), parking and others; fixed, per sq ft or per parking slot; monthly or quarterly) with rent increases (for example 5% every 12 months), 4) Check and save. The app refuses a unit that another lease already holds for the same dates.
- **"Check and save" shows the full rent schedule** worked out by the billing engine, with the same settings the lease will be billed with. What you see there is what gets billed (D20).
- **Lease settings.** "More options for this lease" (on the Rent and charges step) lets one lease differ from the building's billing rules: rounding of increased rent, part-quarter method, month-end rule, and whether automatic increases resume after a custom one.
- **Lease page.** Tenant, units, deposit, when rent starts, each charge with its amount and how it was worked out, the key dates and the rent schedule.
- **Key dates** are generated from the lease: expiry, end of lock-in, the last day to give notice, and every rent increase, each with reminder days. (The reminders themselves are sent from M5/M6.)
- **Copy to a new lease.** Starts a new lease in the same building with the same charges, increases, rent-free period and settings, but no tenant, units, dates or deposit.
- **Renew.** Starts a new version of the lease for the same tenant, beginning the day after the old one ends. Saving it marks the old version "Renewed"; the old version still bills to its own end date.
- **End early.** Enter the tenant's last day and confirm. The lease, its open charges and its expiry date end on that day; later key dates close. Nothing is deleted and no amount is changed (D21). A lease that ended early can't be renewed; it becomes a new lease if the tenant comes back.
- **Behind the scenes.** Six new tables (leases, lease units, charges, rent increase rules, custom increases, key dates), protected by the same organization-only database security as everything else, and every change goes in the activity log. The demo data now has 45 leases.

## How to try it

1. Start Docker Desktop, then `supabase start`, `pnpm db:reset` and `pnpm dev`.
2. Open http://localhost:3000 and **Create an account**. Add a building with a few units (**Leases** → **Buildings and units**) and a tenant (**Tenants** → **Add tenant**).
3. **Leases** → **Add lease**. Pick the tenant and units, set dates with a 2-month rent-free period, add rent with a 5% increase every 12 months, and open **More options for this lease** to see the rules this lease can change. **Next** to **Check and save**: read the schedule (₹0 for the first two months, then the increases). Go **Back**, change a rule, and see the schedule change. **Save lease**.
4. Try adding a second lease on the same unit and dates: the app says the unit is taken.
5. On the lease page: read **Key dates** and **Rent schedule**, then try **Copy to a new lease** and **Renew**.
6. At the bottom of the lease page, enter a **Tenant's last day** → **End lease on this day** → **End lease**. The status becomes "Ended early" and the end date moves.

The seeded demo organization's 45 leases have no login yet; sign up to create your own.

## For the next milestone (M4)

- Load a lease with `getLease` (`src/lib/leases/queries.ts`) and turn it into engine input with `leaseInputFor` (`src/lib/leases/draft.ts`) and the lease's resolved settings (`loadSettings` with `leaseSettings: lease.settings`). Don't write a second path (D20).
- Bill leases with status `active`, `in_notice`, `expired` and `renewed`, each only up to its own end date. A `renewed` version is usually marked so before it ends and must still bill to its end (D20). A `terminated` lease bills to its exit date (its end date).
- Ending a lease early after advance invoices were sent needs a credit note for the days after the exit (D21).
- Charges always have timing, GST rate and SAC saved (D16); invoice lines should carry a stable `key` (charge id + period start) for per-invoice GST rounding (backlog).
- Use `todayInIndia()` (`src/lib/today.ts`) in server code to get today's date for the billing run; the engine never reads the clock.
- Key dates (`critical_dates`) are ready for reminders and the Today screen (M5/M6).

## After the Codex review (2026-10-24)

- A renewal must be for the same building and units; the server refuses other premises ("add a new lease").
- The charges step refuses a charge that ends before it starts, including one with a blank start that ends before the lease starts.
- After saving a lease, "Renew" or "Add lease" used to reopen the saved lease's form on its review step, because Next keeps the page alive between visits. The form now starts fresh.
- New browser test `tests/e2e/lease-versions.spec.ts`: renew a lease, check the old version says Renewed, then end the new version early.
