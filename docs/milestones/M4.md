## In plain words

Each month the app works out every tenant's invoice. You check them, then send them all with one button. A sent invoice is never changed: corrections are credit notes.

- **Make invoices.** Billing → "Make Nov 2026 invoices" works out the month's invoice for every lease. The invoices show as "Not sent yet". If a lease changes, you can make them again. The same run also happens by itself at 6 am on your run day (Settings → Billing rules → "Day to make invoices", the 1st unless you change it).
- **Check each invoice.** The invoice page shows every line. Tap a line to see how its amount and GST were worked out, or tap the total to see how it adds up. "Preview PDF" shows the tax invoice exactly as the tenant will get it.
- **Send all.** "Send 38 invoices" asks you to confirm first. Then it gives each invoice its number (for example `B1/26-27/0001`, with no gaps) and emails the PDF to the tenant's billing contacts.
  - Invoices that can't be sent (no GSTIN, or a prefix too long) are listed with what to fix.
  - Tenants with no email address are named before you confirm, so you can send their PDF yourself.
  - You can also send a single invoice from its own page.
- **The PDF** has the GST fields, bank details and a UPI QR code. The QR asks for the total less the TDS the tenant is expected to deduct. Above ₹1 lakh it carries no amount, because of UPI's payment limit.
- **Email again** re-sends a sent invoice, for example after you fix the tenant's email address.
- **Credit notes.** On a sent invoice, choose "Issue a credit note":
  - Pick why (correction, waiver, discount, other) and add a note.
  - Each line starts at everything that's left on it; lower the amounts to take off less. The total with GST updates as you type.
  - The credit note gets its own number (for example `TCN/26-27/0001`) and a PDF that names the original invoice, and it is emailed to the tenant.
  - Once nothing is left on the invoice, it reads "Credit note issued".
- **Until the Resend account exists**, each email is saved as a file in `.local-outbox/` in the project folder (the message and its PDF), so you can try the whole flow without sending anything.

## How to try it

1. Start Docker Desktop. Run `supabase start`, then `pnpm db:migrate` (this applies the invoice migrations; it only works on your own computer), then `pnpm db:reset` and `pnpm dev`.
2. Open http://localhost:3000 and **Create an account**. Add a GSTIN in **Settings → Business details**, then a building, a tenant with a billing contact's email, and a lease.
3. Go to **Billing** → **Make Oct 2026 invoices**. Open an invoice, tap a line and then the total to read the working, and open **Preview PDF**.
4. Back on Billing, tap **Send 1 invoice** → confirm. Open the `.local-outbox/` folder to see the email and its PDF.
5. On the invoice, tap **Issue a credit note** → choose **Waiver**, take ₹5,000 off a line → **Issue credit note** → confirm. The invoice page now lists the credit note with its PDF.
6. Optional: in **Settings → Billing rules**, set "Day to make invoices" to the 25th. Run `pnpm jobs:dev` and trigger the daily run by hand at http://localhost:8288.

## Fixed after review

The billing and UX reviews found four ways an amount could go wrong. All four are fixed:

- **A month you had fully credited was billed again** if you made that month's invoices again. Now a credited month is left alone.
- **A draft could go out with an old amount.** If a lease was ended early, or its charges or billing rules changed, after its draft was made, the draft was sent as it was. Now sending re-checks each draft against its lease and asks you to make the month again if anything changed.
- **Two buildings could give out the same invoice number.** Now the number format must include the building prefix and the year, and a credit note prefix can't match any building's invoice prefix under the same GSTIN.
- **After a part credit note, the QR code still asked for the full amount.** Now it asks for no fixed amount until D-017 is answered.

Also fixed:

- An invoice can't be sent before its date (the 1st of its month).
- "Send all" names the tenants with no email address before you confirm.
- Several smaller wording and layout fixes from the UX review.

## Settings added

All three are new in M4 and are in `docs/SETTINGS.md`:

- **`invoice.runDay`**, "Day to make invoices" (organization, Settings → Billing rules, default the 1st). The 1st makes that month's invoices. A later day, such as the 25th, makes the next month's ahead of time. Invoices are still dated the 1st of their month.
- **`invoice.numberFormat`** (default `{prefix}/{fy}/{seq}`) and **`invoice.startingNumber`** (default: start at 1 each year). Both are set per business (billing entity). They work in the code but aren't on a settings screen yet (backlog), so for now every series starts at 0001.

## For the next milestone (M5)

- **A payment's status changes.** An invoice's `status` (`sent`, `part_paid`, `paid`, `overdue`, `cancelled`) is the only thing that may change after sending (triggers in `drizzle/0013`). Credit notes count towards "paid" (DATA_MODEL invariants).
- **Matching payments.** The amount to match against is the total less `expected_tds`. A tenant who pays the full total has not deducted TDS (D24). After a credit note, expected TDS still has to be worked out again (D-017, backlog). A credit note on a paid invoice should become an advance on the tenant's ledger if D-019 is A.
- **The PDF and QR on part-paid and credited invoices** ask for no amount. Once payments know the balance, print it (backlog, `src/lib/invoices/document.ts`).
- **Reminder emails** go through `emailDriver()` (`src/lib/email/`). Recurring jobs go next to `monthlyBilling` in `src/lib/jobs/` and are registered in `src/app/api/inngest/route.ts`. Use `runAsSystem` only in background code (D22).
- **Email delivery status** could live in the `reminders` table or a new invoice column (backlog).
- **Before payments can be tested against the database,** the invoice migrations must be applied.
