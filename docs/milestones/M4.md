# M4. Invoicing: what was built

Built 2026-10-23 on branch `m3-leases-schema`, with billing and UX reviews of each part. One human task is still open (a Resend account), and the invoice database migrations (`drizzle/0012`–`0013`) still have to be applied by hand: unattended runs can't approve `pnpm db:migrate`. Until then the invoice screens can't save and the invoice database tests are skipped. Next up is **M5: payments and TDS**.

## In plain words

Each month the app works out every tenant's invoice, lets you check it, and sends it with one button. Corrections are credit notes: a sent invoice is never changed.

- **Make invoices.** Billing → "Make Nov 2026 invoices" works out every lease's invoice for the month. They are "Not sent yet" and can be made again if a lease changes. The same run happens by itself each morning on the organization's run day (`invoice.runDay`).
- **Check each invoice.** The invoice page shows every line. Tap a line to see how its amount and its GST were worked out, or tap the total to see how it adds up. "Preview PDF" shows the tax invoice as the tenant will get it.
- **Send all.** "Send 38 invoices" asks first, then gives each invoice its number (for example `B1/26-27/0001`, no gaps) and emails its PDF to the tenant's billing contacts. Any that can't be sent (no GSTIN, a prefix too long) are listed with what to fix; tenants with no email address are listed so you can send the PDF yourself. A single invoice can be sent from its own page.
- **The PDF** has the GST fields, bank details and a UPI QR code asking for the total less expected TDS.
- **Email again** on a sent invoice re-sends it, for example after fixing the tenant's email address.
- **Credit notes.** On a sent invoice, "Issue a credit note": choose why (correction, waiver, discount, other), add a note, and change how much to take off each line (each starts at all that's left). The total with GST is worked out as you type. It gets its own number (for example `TCN/26-27/0001`), a PDF that names the original invoice, and is emailed to the tenant. When nothing is left, the invoice reads "Credit note issued".
- **Until the Resend account exists**, every email is written to `.local-outbox/` in the project folder (the message and its PDF), so the whole flow can be tried.

## How to try it

1. Run `pnpm db:migrate` (once), then `pnpm db:reset` and `pnpm dev`, and sign up. Add a GSTIN in Settings → Business details, a building, a tenant with a billing contact's email, and a lease.
2. **Billing** → **Make Oct 2026 invoices**. Open an invoice, tap a line and the total to read the working, and open **Preview PDF**.
3. Back on Billing, **Send 1 invoice** → confirm. Open `.local-outbox/` to see the email and PDF.
4. On the invoice, **Issue a credit note** → choose **Waiver**, take ₹5,000 off a line → **Issue credit note** → confirm. The invoice page lists it with its PDF.

## Fixed after review

The billing and UX reviews found four ways money could go wrong; all are fixed:

- An invoice credited in full was billed again if you made the month's invoices again. Now a credited month is left alone.
- A draft made before a lease was ended early (or its charges or billing rules changed) was sent with the old amount. Now sending checks the draft against the lease and asks you to make the month again if anything changed.
- Two buildings could give out the same invoice number. Now the number format must include the building prefix and year, and a credit note prefix can't match an invoice prefix under the same GSTIN.
- After a part credit note, the PDF still asked for the full amount in its QR code. Now it asks for no fixed amount until D-017 is answered.

Also: invoices can't be sent before their date (the 1st), and "Send all" names the tenants with no email address before you confirm.
