## In plain words

Money in is now tracked from the moment it lands in the bank to the TDS certificate at the end of the quarter. A recorded payment is never changed. A mistake is undone with a reversal, and both stay on the record.

- **Record a payment.** Choose the tenant, then type the date and the amount received.
  - The app works out the TDS the tenant probably kept back. For example, ₹1,08,000 received against a ₹1,18,000 invoice means ₹10,000 TDS.
  - It applies the money to the oldest unpaid invoices first. You can change any of it before saving.
  - Each invoice then reads "Part paid" or "Paid".
  - If the money is short by up to ₹1 per invoice after TDS (`tds.tolerancePaise`), the invoice still reads Paid and no reminder asks for the paise (D-022).
- **Extra money is kept as credit.** It shows on the tenant's page, with an "Apply ₹… credit" button that pays their unpaid invoices, oldest first.
- **Reverse a payment** entered by mistake, for example against the wrong tenant. The app asks why. The invoices go back to unpaid, and the payment page offers "Record the correct payment".
- **Import a bank statement** (CSV or Excel).
  - The app guesses which column is the date, description and amount, and shows a preview. You confirm or correct it.
  - It remembers the columns for that bank account, so the next statement imports in one step.
  - Only money received is kept. Payments out are left out, and a line already imported from an overlapping statement isn't added twice.
- **Match payments.** Each bank line gets a suggested tenant and invoices. The suggestion is based on:
  - the invoice number in the reference
  - an amount equal to the invoices less TDS
  - the tenant's name
  - "Likely" matches start ticked. "Record N payments" records them all at once. "Mark as not rent" sets a line aside.
  - Nothing is recorded until you confirm.
- **TDS certificates.** The page lists, for each tenant and quarter (April–June and so on), the TDS by our records and whether the certificate (Form 16A) is in.
  - A missing certificate shows as overdue after its due date (15 Aug, 15 Nov, 15 Feb, 15 Jun).
  - Uploading the PDF marks that quarter received.
- **Automatic reminders.** Every morning at 9 am the app emails tenants whose invoices are due soon or overdue: 3 days before the due date, then 1, 7 and 15 days after.
  - The email asks for what is still left less the TDS still expected, so it always matches the payment form.
  - A reminder is never sent twice.
  - You can change the days, or turn reminders off, for everyone or for one tenant.
- **One-tap WhatsApp reminder.** On an unpaid invoice, "Remind {name} on WhatsApp" opens WhatsApp with the message already written. You still press Send. Each tap is recorded on the invoice.
- **Tenant ledger.** On a tenant's page, "See the ledger" lists every invoice, credit note, payment, TDS amount and reversal, oldest first, with a running balance.

## How to try it

1. Start Docker Desktop. Run `supabase start`, then `pnpm db:migrate` and `pnpm dev`. The demo data has no sent invoices yet, so start from a new account as in M4: open http://localhost:3000, **Create an account**, then add a GSTIN, a building, a tenant (with a billing email and a WhatsApp number) and a lease. Then **Billing → Make invoices → Send 1 invoice**.
2. **Record a payment:** go to **Billing → Payments → Record payment**. Choose the tenant and type the invoice total less 10% of the rent. The TDS fills in by itself. Tap **Record payment**. The invoice now reads Paid.
3. **Reverse it:** open the payment and tap **Reverse this payment**. Give a reason, then use **Record the correct payment**.
4. **Ledger and credit:** on the tenant's page, tap **See the ledger**. Record a payment larger than what is owed, and the tenant page offers **Apply ₹… credit** once a new invoice is sent.
5. **Bank statement:** go to **Billing → Payments → Import bank statement**. Add the bank account, choose a CSV or Excel file that has a line with the invoice number in it, check the columns, and import. Then open **Match payments**, tick the line and tap **Record 1 payment**.
6. **TDS:** go to **Billing → Payments → TDS certificates**. Pick the quarter, then upload a PDF for the tenant.
7. **Reminders:** on an unpaid invoice, tap **Remind … on WhatsApp**. For email reminders, set the days in **Settings → Billing rules**. Then run `pnpm jobs:dev` and trigger `daily-payment-reminders` at http://localhost:8288. The email appears in `.local-outbox/`.

## Fixed after review

The billing review found these ways an amount or status could go wrong. All are fixed:

- **TDS from a bank line could be worked out against the wrong invoices.** This happened when the bank named only a newer invoice. Now it uses the older invoices first, the same way a typed payment does.
- **A credit note that cleared a part-paid invoice** left it reading "Part paid". It now reads Paid.
- **An invoice short by a few paise after TDS** stayed "Part paid", and reminders would have asked the tenant for paise. It now reads Paid (D-022).
- **A minus or bracketed amount in a bank file** could be read as money received. It is now shown as a row the app couldn't read.
- **Confirming a bank suggestion after its invoices changed** went ahead with the old ones. It now refuses and asks you to look again.
- **A reminder could be left stuck "sending"** if building the email failed. The email is now built before the reminder is claimed.
- **WhatsApp links** now add India's +91 to a mobile number saved without it.

The UX review's must-fix items are fixed too:

- The tenant page lists the tenant's leases.
- The bank import says "payments received" instead of "credits", and its buttons are clearer.
- Tap targets are bigger.
- A new tenant's state starts as "Choose a state" instead of Maharashtra.
- The next step after a reversal is shown.

The Codex review found that the reminder migrations weren't applied and that their tests skipped instead of failing. They are now applied, and the tests fail if a table is missing.

## Settings added

All three are in `docs/SETTINGS.md` and on **Settings → Billing rules**:

- **`reminders.beforeDueDays`**, days before the due date to remind. Default: 3. Leave it blank for no reminder before the due date.
- **`reminders.afterDueDays`**, days after the due date. Default: 1, 7 and 15.
- **`reminders.autoSend`**, whether reminders go by themselves. Default: on. It can also be turned off for one tenant, under "More options" on the tenant form, for key tenants you'd rather chase yourself.

`tds.tolerancePaise` (from M2) is now used by payments, bank matching and invoice status.

## Decisions

These are recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md`. No action is needed:

- **D29:** a payment is undone by a reversal entry, never edited. Every change to a tenant's money is locked so that two changes can't clash.
- **D30:** bank statements are read in your browser, and only money received is kept. The file itself isn't stored. Excel files are read with SheetJS.
- **D31:** bank lines are matched by a score and recorded only when you confirm.
- **D32:** uploaded files go into one private storage area, with a folder per organization.
- **D33:** each reminder is claimed in the database before it is emailed, so it is never sent twice.
- **D34:** a bank statement import is one entry in the activity log, not one per line.

These are waiting for you in `docs/DECISIONS_NEEDED.md`. None of them blocks work: each has a safe choice built in the meantime.
