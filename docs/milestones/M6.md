## In plain words

Today is now the home screen. It answers "what needs doing?" in one look, and lets you do most of it without leaving the page.

- **Three numbers at the top**, for this month:
  - **Billed:** the total of this month's invoices that have been sent.
  - **Collected:** money received this month, with any TDS the tenants kept back shown underneath. A reversed payment doesn't count.
  - **Unpaid now:** everything still owed on sent invoices, with the overdue part underneath. It is worked out the same way as the payment form, so the two always agree.
- **Needs attention**, a list of things to act on, oldest first:
  - **Tenants who haven't paid**, one row per tenant. It asks for what is left less the TDS they may still keep back, the same amount as the reminder emails. Buttons: **Remind on WhatsApp** and **Open invoice**.
  - **This month's invoices aren't made yet**, with a **Make invoices** button. It doesn't show in a month where no lease bills anything, such as quarterly leases between quarters.
  - **Invoices not sent yet**, with a **Send** button.
  - **Bank payments to match** and **TDS certificates missing**, which before could only be found under Billing → Payments.
  - **Lease dates coming up:** a lease ending, a rent increase, a lock-in ending. They show from the first reminder day (for example 180 days before a lease ends). Each has **Mark as done** and **Remind me later** (hides it for 7 days).
  - After a button works, the item goes away and a short message above the list says what happened.
- **Daily email to the team.** Every morning at 8 am, each person gets an email with their items from Today.
  - Owners and accountants get the money items. Lease dates go to the person they are assigned to, otherwise to owners and property managers. Viewers get nothing.
  - Money items are in every morning's email while they are open. A lease date is emailed only on its reminder days.
  - Nobody gets an empty email, and nobody gets two in a day.
  - It can be turned off for everyone in **Settings → Billing rules**.

## How to try it

1. Start Docker Desktop. Run `supabase start`, then `pnpm db:migrate` and `pnpm dev`. Open http://localhost:3000 and **Create an account**. (The demo data has no login yet; that comes with M5a.)
2. With no leases, **Today** says there's nothing yet. Add a GSTIN, a building, a unit, a tenant and a lease starting this month, as in M4.
3. Go back to **Today**. It says "<month> invoices aren't made yet". Tap **Make <month> invoices**. The item changes to "1 invoice not sent yet". Tap **Send 1 invoice**, then confirm. The **Billed** number now shows the invoice total.
4. To see an unpaid tenant: send an invoice whose due date has passed (for example, a lease that started last month). The tenant appears with **Remind on WhatsApp**.
5. To see lease dates: add a lease ending within the next 180 days. It appears with **Mark as done** and **Remind me later**. The days are under **Settings → Billing rules → More options**.
6. **Daily email:** run `pnpm jobs:dev`, open http://localhost:8288 and trigger `daily-digest`. The email appears in `.local-outbox/`. Triggering it again the same day sends nothing more.

## Fixed after review

- **"Invoices aren't made yet" stuck forever** (and was emailed daily) in a month where no lease bills anything, such as quarterly leases between quarters. It now shows only when some lease does bill that month.
- **A lease date assigned to a viewer** would have been emailed to them. It now goes to owners and property managers instead.
- **Totals too large to add up safely** are now refused with an error rather than rounded. In practice no landlord comes near this limit, but money must never be quietly wrong (Codex review).
- Plainer words: "TDS certificate" instead of "Form 16A" on Today, "Saving…" instead of "Snoozing…", "accountants" in the email setting.
- One filled (main) button on Today, even with two months of unsent invoices.
- Card-shaped placeholders while Today loads, instead of the word "Loading…" (Codex review).

## Settings added

All are in `docs/SETTINGS.md` and on **Settings → Billing rules**:

- **`alerts.dailyDigest`**, "Daily email to the team". Default: on.
- Already listed, now working (under "More options"):
  - **`alerts.expiryDays`**, "Remind before a lease ends". Default: 180, 90 and 30 days.
  - **`alerts.increaseDays`**, "Remind before a rent increase". Default: 30 and 7 days.
  - **`alerts.lockInDays`**, "Remind before a lock-in ends". Default: 60 days.
  - Changing these updates every lease date not yet done, not only leases saved afterwards.

## Backlog (`docs/BACKLOG.md`)

Nothing here gives a wrong amount. The M6 items:

- **Daily email:** a failed email isn't tried again that day. There is no way for one person to turn it off for themselves yet, only for everyone.
- **Today screen:**
  - If one item's lookup fails (say one tenant's WhatsApp link), the whole page shows an error. It should drop just that button.
  - "Mark as done" has no undo.
  - No "Remind all" button for many unpaid tenants. The automatic reminder emails already chase them.
  - The amounts can't yet be tapped to see how they were worked out.
  - The message above the list never clears, and is green even when it lists a problem.
  - Wording and layout tidy-ups from the UX review, and clearer labels for some older settings.
- **Speed:** Today checks tenants and leases one at a time. Fine at 40 tenants, slow at a few hundred. Batch it before pilots with large portfolios.
- **Tests:** a database test for a lease that ended last month with a quarter still to bill.
- **Still from M5:** nothing marks an invoice "Overdue" yet. Today and the reminders go by the due date instead, so you see the same thing either way.
