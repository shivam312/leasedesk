## In plain words

### Reports

**Reports** in the bottom menu now lists seven reports. Each has a date or month filter, a search box, and **Download Excel** and **Download PDF** buttons. The files always show exactly what is on screen.

- **Rent roll:** every lease, with its tenant, units, billed area, rent and other charges per month, rent per sq ft, key dates (rent start, lock-in, end), the next rent increase and the rent after it, and the deposit. The rent is worked out by the same code that makes invoices, so it always matches what tenants are billed.
- **Vacancy:** which units are empty on a date and since when, and the empty area of each building.
- **Expiry schedule:** leases ending in the next 12 months, grouped by financial-year quarter, with the last day to give notice and the rent in the final month.
- **Collections:** money received between two dates, by tenant. A bounced cheque is taken off.
- **Unpaid and ageing:** what each tenant owes today, split into "not yet due", 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and over 90 days overdue. It uses the same sums as Today, so the two always agree.
- **GST summary:** a month's sent invoices and credit notes by GSTIN and GST rate, split into B2B (tenant has a GSTIN) and B2C. The figures are copied from the invoices as printed, never worked out again. It is a help for your accountant's GST returns, not a filing.
- **TDS summary:** TDS each tenant deducted, by quarter, with their PAN, how much is covered by a TDS certificate, what is still pending and when it is due.

In Excel, amounts are real numbers in Indian format (₹1,18,000.00) and dates are real dates (DD-MM-YYYY), so an accountant can add up and filter them. The PDF is A4 landscape with page numbers.

### Tally export

**Reports → Tally export** makes a file your accountant imports into TallyPrime, for any two dates:

- each sent invoice as a Sales voucher, each credit note as a Credit Note voucher
- each payment as a Receipt: bank and TDS on one side, the tenant on the other, against the invoices it paid
- a bounced cheque as a Journal that undoes its receipt; credit applied later as its own Journal
- every voucher must add up to the paisa, or no file is made.

The ledger names (Rent Income, Output CGST, TDS Receivable and so on) are set on **Settings → Tally**, and a tenant's name in Tally can differ from its name here. Each download is recorded. If you download a period that overlaps an earlier file, the screen warns you and the button reads **Download Tally file anyway**, so nothing is entered in Tally twice by mistake. Accountants who don't use Tally can **Download as Excel** instead.

**The file hasn't been checked against a real TallyPrime yet.** It follows Tally's published import format. Please get a sample export (see Human tasks) before an accountant relies on it.

### Import from Excel

**Leases → Import from Excel** lets a landlord move from their spreadsheet in a day:

1. **Buildings and units:** download the template, fill in the Buildings and Units sheets, upload.
2. **Tenants and leases:** download the second template, fill in the Tenants and Leases sheets (one lease per row: units, dates, lock-in, notice, rent-free, deposit, rent per sq ft or per month, monthly or quarterly, one yearly increase rule, maintenance and parking), upload.

Every row goes through the same checks as the forms. Problems are listed with the sheet and the row number Excel shows, for example "Units, row 7: …", followed by what is wrong. **If any row has a problem, nothing is saved**, so you never end up with half a file imported. Fix the file and upload it again.

The rent column is **"Rent at lease start (₹)"**. If a rent increase should already have been billed, **"Rent today (₹)"** must also be filled in and must match what LeaseDesk works out. This stops a landlord who types today's rent as the starting rent from being billed the past increases a second time.

What tenants already owe from before isn't imported yet (D-028).

## How to try it

1. Start Docker Desktop. Run `supabase start`, then `pnpm db:migrate` and `pnpm dev`. Open http://localhost:3000 and **Create an account**. (The demo data has no login yet; that comes with M5a.) Add a GSTIN under **Settings → Business details**.
2. **Import:** go to **Leases → Import from Excel**. Tap **Download the buildings and units template (.xlsx)**, fill in one building and two units, and tap **Import buildings and units**. Then do the same with the tenants and leases template and **Import tenants and leases**. Try a wrong date in one row first to see the row message, and see that nothing was saved.
3. **Reports:** go to **Billing**, tap **Make invoices**, then send them. Open **Reports**, pick **Rent roll**, then **Download Excel** and **Download PDF**. Try each of the other reports.
4. **Tally:** open **Settings → Tally** and look at the ledger names. Record a payment, then go to **Reports → Tally export**, pick the month and tap **Download Tally file**. Download it again: the warning appears.

## Fixed after review

- **Lease import rent typed as today's rent** would have had past increases added again. The column is now "Rent at lease start", with "Rent today" checked against the engine (billing review).
- **A whole monthly rent typed in the per sq ft column** is refused (over ₹2,000 per sq ft a month) with a pointer to the "Rent is" column.
- **Increases counted from rent start** can now be imported, as the lease form allows.
- **Imported quarterly rent** typed per month is billed as three months a quarter; **Excel percent cells** (5%) are read as typed.
- **Expiry schedule** no longer fails on a notice day the month doesn't have (31 May less 3 months).
- **Credit applied later** goes to Tally as its own Journal, so an old month's file never changes.
- **Negative amounts in Excel** show their minus sign with lakh grouping.
- **Two tenants with the same company name** are no longer added together in Collections and Unpaid and ageing (Codex review).
- After an import, a link to the next step; empty reports say what to do; 44px buttons on phones (UX review).
