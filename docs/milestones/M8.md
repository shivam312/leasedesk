## In plain words

### Knowing about errors before landlords do

When something breaks on a screen or in a background job, LeaseDesk now reports it to Sentry, an error-tracking service, so you see it before a landlord messages you. It is off until you create a Sentry account and put its key in the settings; until then errors are only written to the app's log, as before.

Reports say what failed and which record (an invoice number or an id), never a tenant's name, GSTIN or an amount. Sentry has no India data centre, so this keeps tenant and payment details from leaving India. A broken page shows "Something went wrong" with a **Try again** button instead of a blank screen.

### Forgotten password

**Log in → Forgot your password?** asks for an email and sends a link. The link opens a page to choose a new password (at least 8 characters, typed twice), then goes to Today. The answer is the same whether or not the email has an account, so nobody can use the form to find out who your customers are. An expired or already-used link says so and offers to send a new one. Before this, an owner who forgot their password had no way back in.

### One test for the whole month

A new browser test does what a pilot landlord does in their first month, start to finish: create an account, import two tenants and their leases from Excel, make and send both invoices, record a payment with TDS, then check that Rent roll, Unpaid and ageing, Collections, the GST and TDS summaries, and Today all show the same figures. The invoice, GST and TDS amounts in it were worked out by hand, and Codex checked them again independently.

## How to try it

1. Start Docker Desktop. Run `supabase start`, then `pnpm db:migrate` and `pnpm dev`. Open http://localhost:3000 and **Create an account**.
2. **Password reset:** log out. On the login page tap **Forgot your password?**, type your email and tap **Email me a link**. Open Mailpit at http://127.0.0.1:54324 (it catches every email on your computer), open the email and tap the link. Choose a new password and tap **Save new password**; you land on Today. Log out and log in with the new password.
3. **Error reporting:** nothing to see without a Sentry account. Once `SENTRY_DSN` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_SENTRY_DSN` are set, an error on any screen appears in Sentry within a minute.
4. **The whole-month test:** run `pnpm test:e2e journey` and watch it pass (it takes a minute or two).

## Fixed after review

- **Error reports could carry tenant details:** a failed database save puts the values it tried to save (names, GSTINs, amounts) in its error text. Reports now keep only the kind of error, and drop page addresses' query strings and the trail of earlier clicks (billing review).
- **Error reports could be lost** when a server function stopped right after answering. They are now sent before it stops (Codex review).
- **A reset link could be bent to send someone to another website** by hiding a tab or line break in it. Refused now (billing review).
- **A reset page left open until the session ended** failed with a confusing message. It now says the link expired and offers a new one.
- **Plainer reset and invite pages:** a link to ask for a new reset link, a "check your spam folder" tip, who to ask for a new invite (UX review).
- **The whole-month test promised to check Today's collected money but didn't.** It now checks ₹1,08,000 collected and ₹10,000 TDS kept back (billing review).
- **The roadmap claimed every screen had a UX review.** It now lists the screens that had one, and the rest is an open task (Codex review, D-030).

## Tests added

- **Unit and database tests:** 945 in total, up from 927 at the end of M7, so 18 are new. All pass, with none skipped. They cover:
  - what goes into an error report: no error text, request body, cookies, query strings or breadcrumbs, only ids (`src/lib/monitoring/__tests__/monitoring.test.ts`)
  - that reports are sent before a server function stops, and nothing is sent without a DSN
  - the password reset forms, and that a reset link can only lead to a page inside the app (`src/lib/auth/__tests__/forms.test.ts`)
- **Browser tests:** `tests/e2e/journey.spec.ts` (new organization → import → billing run → send → payment → reports and Today) and `tests/e2e/auth.spec.ts` (forgot password, the emailed link read from Mailpit, a new password, logging in with it, an expired session), at phone and laptop widths.

## Settings added

None. Error reporting is switched on by account keys (`SENTRY_DSN`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_SENTRY_DSN`), not by a setting a landlord changes.

## Backlog (`docs/BACKLOG.md`)

Nothing here gives a wrong amount on an invoice. The M8 items:

- **Do before a pilot (UX review must-fixes):**
  - A tenant's contact can't be edited or removed, or chosen as the one who gets invoices, so a mistyped email sends invoices and reminders to the wrong place.
  - A pending invite's link is shown once and can't be copied again or cancelled, and a team member can't be removed.
- **Billing:** "Email again" sends in one tap without saying to whom; a month's invoice list has no search, status filter or "Send selected"; the credit note's two buttons have the same name.
- **Log in and passwords:** no word after a password is changed; no "Show password" on phones; a failed login doesn't point to "Forgot your password?".
- **Tenants and leases:** the tenant page doesn't list the unpaid invoices, and the ledger has no **Record payment** or year filter; on a phone, the lease form doesn't scroll to the top of the next step; the lease list has no rent column or "Ending soon" filter; units don't say who rents them.
- **Buildings and settings:** no suggested invoice prefix for a new building; no "← Settings" link on most settings pages.
- **Payments and TDS:** Match payments has no filter or search for a long statement; small wording and alignment fixes on the TDS tracker.
- **Tests:** the whole-month test checks report amounts anywhere on the page rather than in their row and column.
