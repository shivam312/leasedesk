## In plain words

### The demo works as a demo should

- **No red badge.** Next.js's development warning that appeared on every click is gone, and the demo script now says to run the demo on a production build (`pnpm build && pnpm start`), which is also faster.
- **Today never opens empty.** `pnpm db:seed:demo` seeds the current month as already sent on the 1st, with some of the payments in, so the top of Today shows real figures whatever day you demo. The demo makes next month's invoices instead.
- **Overdue means overdue.** Nothing in the app had ever set an invoice to "Overdue"; a 46-day-late invoice read "Sent". The label is now worked out from the due date and what's still to pay, in one place, so the invoice list, invoice page, tenant page, ledger and Today all agree.
- **Today is shorter.** Instead of 40 cards, it lists the 5 oldest unpaid tenants with a total and "See all N". Rent increases falling on the same day share one card. The amount a tenant "hasn't paid" says it is after expected TDS, and the "Unpaid now" box and the Unpaid and ageing report use the same gross figure, so the two screens agree.
- **The demo shows a rent increase.** Part 4 of the demo adds a 5% yearly increase, and the lease form suggests the day before the anniversary as the last day of the lease.
- **Dates read the Indian way.** DD-MM-YYYY is shown beside every date picker (lease form, payment form, reports' "As on", Tally export).
- **Smaller demo fixes:** once a month's invoices are all sent, the big button is no longer "Make invoices" (only "Make missing … invoices", and only when a lease has none); on a phone the lease page shows the next 12 months of the schedule then "Show all N months"; invoice lines come in one fixed order, rent first, the same on screen and PDF; the tenant's address doesn't repeat the state; building cards count empty units from running leases and show an occupancy %, with fit-out units counted separately, so the card and the Vacancy report agree.

### A real landlord's first month

- **Credit notes can't be issued by accident.** Every line now starts at ₹0 and the total is shown before "Issue credit note". Before, an accountant waiving ₹5,000 who didn't retype each line would have credited the whole invoice, irreversibly.
- **Email fails closed.** On a real server without a Resend key, "Send all", "Send" and "Email again" refuse with "Email isn't set up yet" and the invoices stay drafts. Settings → Business shows the same. Before, invoices looked sent and went to a folder on the server. Locally (`pnpm dev`) nothing changes: emails still land in `.local-outbox/`.
- **Tenant contacts can be fixed.** On the tenant page you can edit or remove a contact and choose who receives invoices; the app won't let you remove the last contact with an email. "Add a contact" sits behind a plain button.
- **Team members can be removed.** On Settings → Team an owner can remove a member or cancel a pending invite, with a confirmation; both are logged.
- **The Tally file imports.** It now writes the party ledgers (and the TDS and bank ledgers it posts to) ahead of the vouchers, so TallyPrime doesn't refuse it when a ledger doesn't exist yet. Each tenant's Tally name is saved at the first export, so renaming a tenant later doesn't split their postings. The Tally page has a business (GSTIN) filter, one file per Tally company.
- **You know if the daily job stops.** Every daily run is recorded. If it hasn't happened for more than a day, Today and Settings say "Automatic billing hasn't run since …" with a "Run now" button.
- **A deploy runbook and CI.** `docs/DEPLOY.md` walks through the first deploy, releases and rollback, cloud migrations, environment variables, Inngest, Storage policies, backups and a restore drill, all as human steps. `.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs typecheck, lint, every unit and database test and every browser test on each push, once the repository is on GitHub.
- **Jobs can't cross organizations.** Every background job path (billing, reminders, digest, job health, the demo reset) was audited and now has tests that run two organizations side by side and check nothing of one touches the other. One real fix came out of it: resetting the demo deleted every organization called "Sharma Estates", not just the demo's.
- **The invoice list is workable with 45 tenants.** Search by tenant or building, status chips, tick rows and "Send selected"; the phone cards show the invoice number.
- **"Remind all" on Today** emails every overdue tenant with an address in one go, asking for the oldest invoice's own amount after TDS, and lists who has no email. A failed email can be tried again the same day.
- **Tenant statement.** The ledger has financial-year chips, "Download statement (PDF)" and a "Record payment" button, and the tenant page lists the tenant's invoices with status and a link to each.
- **Reminders are shown before they're switched on.** Settings → Billing rules previews the reminder emails and their dates for a sample invoice.
- **The PDF a tenant got is the PDF you download later.** Sending stores the PDF; later downloads and "Email again" serve that file while the invoice is still unpaid and uncredited. Once it's part-paid, paid, cancelled or credited, the PDF is rendered afresh so it carries the PAID or CANCELLED notice and drops the payment QR.

### The v1 promises that hadn't been kept

- **Documents.** Upload the signed agreement, addenda, ID proof or insurance on the lease page, and documents on the tenant page; open them from there. Files go straight from the browser to Storage, so the limit rose from 3 MB to 25 MB, with a clear message when a file is too big.
- **Activity.** The tenant, lease, invoice and payment pages show who changed what and when.
- **Key dates.** Add your own dates to a lease, and mark a lease-end alert "In discussion" so Today stops repeating it until the last reminder window.
- **"Request from tenant"** on a missing TDS certificate emails the billing contact in one tap and records when they were asked. It is offered only once the certificate is overdue, for the part not yet covered.
- **Search.** A search box in the header finds tenants, units and invoice numbers. The Payments list and the TDS tracker have a search and a month or quarter filter; Match payments has a search.
- **Deposits as a ledger.** Each lease has a deposit ledger: received, refunded, kept. Nothing can go out beyond what was held on that date. On exit, a settlement statement PDF shows dues after expected TDS, less the deposit held, after the tenant's unapplied payment credit, and says what it doesn't include (unbilled charges). Using the deposit to pay unpaid rent is refused for now with a message saying what to do instead (D-034). No GST invoice is raised on a kept deposit (D-035, **[CA]**).
- **"Log in with Google"** appears on the login page only when the Supabase project's Google provider is turned on (a human task).
- **Paste units from Excel** on the building page: rows of unit code, floor and area.
- **Smaller:** TDS certificates have their own entry under Billing; the rent roll has phone cards headed by the unit; the SAC code is a quiet detail line on screen (still on the PDF); "Round off", "Billed area" and "Charged as" have plain-language help; the invoice page says when the UPI QR has no amount (over ₹1,00,000) or is left off (paid, part-paid); an unused Vitest config file was deleted and old setup notes moved to `docs/archive/`.

## How to try it

1. Start Docker Desktop. Run `supabase start`, `pnpm db:migrate`, then `pnpm db:seed:demo` and note the password it prints. Run `pnpm build && EMAIL_DRIVER=console pnpm start` and log in at http://localhost:3000 as `demo@sharma-estates.example`.
2. **Today:** the 5 oldest unpaid tenants with a total card and "See all"; "Remind all N" above them emails every overdue tenant and then says who had no address (the emails are in `.local-outbox/`). An overdue invoice reads "Overdue".
3. **Billing:** type a tenant's name in the search box, tap a status chip, tick two draft rows and use "Send selected". Open an invoice and read the note under the QR. Tap **Credit note**: every line starts at ₹0 and the total updates as you type.
4. **A tenant** (Tenants → any): the **Contacts** section has Edit and Remove on each contact and "Receives invoices"; the **Invoices** list shows status with a link to each; **Documents** and **Activity** are below. Tap **Ledger**: financial-year chips, **Download statement (PDF)**, **Record payment**.
5. **A lease** (from the tenant page): **Documents** to upload the agreement; **Key dates** with **Add a date** and "In discussion" on the lease end; **Deposit** with **Record a deposit entry** and **Download the deposit settlement statement (PDF)**. At 375px the schedule shows 12 months, then "Show all N months".
6. **Billing → TDS certificates:** on an overdue missing certificate, **Request from tenant**; the row then says when they were asked. Try the search and quarter chips.
7. **Header:** type a tenant, unit or invoice number into the search box.
8. **Settings → Team:** invite someone, then **Cancel invite**; **Remove** on a member. **Settings → Billing rules:** scroll to the reminder preview. **Settings → Business:** the email line says whether sending is set up.
9. **Reports → Tally export:** pick a business (GSTIN) and download; open the XML and see the `LEDGER` entries before the vouchers.
10. **Buildings:** the card shows occupancy %; open a building and tap **Paste units from Excel**.
11. **Job health:** `pnpm test:e2e job-health` shows the warning and "Run now"; on the demo it appears only if the daily job hasn't recorded a run for more than a day.

## Fixed after review

The billing and UX reviewers found these on 2026-10-24; all are fixed:

- **A paid invoice's stored PDF still asked for money.** The send-time PDF is served only while the invoice is still sent and uncredited; after that it renders afresh with the PAID or CANCELLED notice and no QR (billing review).
- **"Remind all" asked for the tenant's whole balance** on the oldest invoice. It now asks for that invoice's own amount, and a failed email can be retried the same day (billing review).
- **The settlement statement ignored the tenant's unapplied credit.** It settles dues from that credit first, then the deposit, shows credit left over as also to pay back, says the credit is the tenant's across all their leases, and says unbilled charges aren't included (billing review, twice).
- **A deposit refund was checked against today's total,** so a backdated refund could exceed what was held on its date. Entries are replayed in date order; an entry already short on its own doesn't block every later one (billing review, twice).
- **"Request from tenant" trusted a hidden form field** for the TDS amount. It is worked out on the server, and asked only once the certificate is overdue, for the part not yet covered (billing review, twice).
- **A failed Google login said nothing.** The login page now says so (UX review).
- **Wording and tap targets:** the tenant page's documents empty state links to Billing → TDS certificates; the deposit form says entries can't be edited or deleted; "Automatic billing hasn't run since …"; 44px buttons (UX review).
- **Demo seed deleted any organization named "Sharma Estates".** Only the one the demo login belongs to now (isolation audit, D50).
