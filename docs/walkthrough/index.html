A month at Sharma Estates

Recorded on 24-10-2026 by pnpm walkthrough, on the demo data from pnpm db:seed:demo. All names, GSTINs and amounts are made up.

What a landlord's month looks like in LeaseDesk: checking Today, looking up a tenant, adding a lease, making and sending Oct 2026's invoices, recording payments by hand and from a bank statement, and the reports. Every number on these screens was worked out by the app from the leases; the captions say which rule or setting produced it (rules are in docs/BILLING_RULES.md, settings in docs/SETTINGS.md).

Videos of the whole run: on a laptop and on a phone. They're made fresh by pnpm walkthrough and aren't kept in git, so run it once to see them.
